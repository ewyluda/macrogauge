## F1 — P2: The headline turns a tracked estimate into an unqualified market-wide fact

**Location:** `site/src/lib/capacityCohort.ts:41–43`.

The new H1 says “Hyperscalers run 91% of the 41.5 GW of AI capacity live today.” Its denominator is the de-duplicated sum of this tracker’s curated companies, not a census of all operational AI capacity. The function loses both the tracked-universe qualification and the uncertainty in the inputs.

The current config makes that uncertainty explicit: Amazon’s 15 GW operational figure is marked an estimate with a 12–20 GW range; Google and Meta disclose no consolidated MW figure and have analyst-derived estimates; Microsoft’s flag distinguishes a broad AI-capable definition from AI-specific chips. Of the 37,721 operational hyperscaler MW in the headline numerator, 34,571 MW are in rows whose confidence is `estimate`. The curate date is October 2, while the new sentence says “live today.” These limitations are known in the data; this is not a challenge to the arithmetic or a fresh estimate of the actual global share.

The earlier question-style H1 did not assert a measured worldwide share. The new default scatter also suppresses hyperscaler rows, so readers cannot see their Estimate badges without switching views.

**Correction:** describe approximately 91% of *tracked, estimated operational MW*, and retain the curated as-of context. A suitable sentence is “Hyperscalers account for about 91% of our estimated 41.5 GW of tracked operational capacity.”

## F2 — P2: The new valuation sentence hides a market-cap-versus-EV comparison

**Location:** `site/src/lib/capacityCohort.ts:44–47`.

The sentence says the companies “are worth $243B combined” and Nvidia “is worth 24 times that.” The numerator is Nvidia’s equity market capitalization; the denominator is the cohort’s enterprise value, which adds net debt. The new prose labels neither basis and reads as a like-for-like company-value comparison. The existing KPI explicitly names both bases; the new headline discards that distinction.

**Offline reproduction with the current config and the same committed quotes:**

| Input or result | Value |
| --- | ---: |
| Included companies | 14 |
| Combined equity market capitalization | $206.74B |
| Combined enterprise value, rounded by publisher | $242.5B |
| Nvidia equity market capitalization | $5,735.1B |
| Nvidia market cap / cohort EV | 23.65×, displayed as 24× |
| Nvidia market cap / cohort market cap | 27.74×, approximately 28× |

**Correction:** either name the differing bases in the sentence (“Nvidia’s market cap is … times their combined EV”) or calculate a consistently defined equity-to-equity or EV-to-EV comparison. Do not change the metric silently.

## F3 — P2: Searching an excluded company bypasses its withholding explanation

**Locations:** `site/src/components/capacity/ValuationScatter.tsx:35–36,192`; new suppression at `pipeline/publish/capacity.py:152`.

Once the new data is published, searching for `MARA`, `AKAM`, or `EQIX` on the default valuation tab leaves one valid company with a null `ev_per_mw`. The component returns immediately when `pts` is empty, before rendering the new exclusion note. It says the value is withheld “for hyperscalers and private builders,” although these are public non-hyperscaler companies, and does not display the searched company’s curated reason.

**Evidence:** server-rendering the actual component with each schema-validated regenerated row produces the same generic paragraph; none includes its `ev_note`. MARA’s explicit bitcoin-mining/no-AI-lease reason is lost. This is a newly reachable state for these three otherwise-priced rows.

**Correction:** render excluded-company reasons even when there are no plotted points, with separate wording for no search matches and missing quotes. The new `ev_note` handling must precede or participate in the early return.

## F4 — P3: The new browser test passes without exercising any exclusion

**Location:** `site/e2e/research-refresh.spec.ts:123–128`.

The test selects only rows already carrying `ev_note` and asserts inside a loop. The committed `capacity.json` has no such rows, so it performs **zero suppression assertions**. The full browser suite passes while AKAM, MARA, and EQIX remain plotted at $383.2M, $236.8M, and $123.6M per weighted MW respectively. The test also checks only null data and presence in explanatory text, not absence from chart marks/table rows.

**Correction:** exercise a fixture generated from the new config, assert the expected excluded tickers exist, and verify their absence from the chart/table plus their individual empty-search explanations. If the production sample intentionally remains on the old contract until the daily run, label that test state explicitly instead of claiming this branch was tested.
