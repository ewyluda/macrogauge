## P2 — Fix the retirement boundary before collecting a returning endpoint

Location: `pipeline/publish/compute.py:391-392`; `_model_rows` at 263-265 and `_index` at 215-217 explain the behavior.

The new `TOKEN_DISPLAY_ONLY` set is passed to `_index` as `retired`, but that flag only clips each series after its **latest observation in the current store**. The PR deliberately keeps DeepSeek's endpoint registered, so when it returns the new observation moves that boundary forward. The model automatically participates again, including being carried through its previous absence, despite its row still declaring `in_index: false`. This contradicts the documented requirement that re-admission needs a separate roster decision and can restate already-published index levels.

The offline probe starts all seven models at 1 on October 16–17, doubles the six continuing models on October 18, and leaves DeepSeek absent. October 18 has six members and index 200. Adding only a returning DeepSeek quote of 4 on October 20 changes October 18 to seven members and index **181.145**, and October 20 to **220.818**. The table still marks DeepSeek outside the index.

Use an explicit retirement date/cutoff for index history while allowing subsequent observations in the display row. Add a regression that collects a returning maker quote, verifies historical and current membership remain unchanged after retirement, and separately verifies the displayed quote updates. Do not remove all historical DeepSeek observations, which would change pre-retirement history.
