## Findings in severity order

### 1. P2 — Synchronize the unmapped site inventory with the new 800 MW ramp

**Diff location:** `config/capacity.json:3254–3258`. The missed counterpart is `config/capacity.json:5184–5188`.

The PR replaces the former 400 MW construction phase with an 800 MW Q4 ramp, explicitly absorbing the old phase. However, `geo_unmapped` still contains only the old `Colossus 2 - next phase (>=220k GB300)` row at 400 MW. The publisher passes that array through unchanged, so regenerating the artifact does not reconcile the two representations.

**Reproduction:** at the base, both company construction and geographic construction inventory are 400 MW. At the reviewed head, the company and its Q4 timeline publish 800 MW, while all XAI construction rows across `geo` and `geo_unmapped` still sum to 400 MW. In the regenerated browser page, `/capacity?tab=Timeline&q=XAI` shows **+800 MW** in Q4 2026; switching to **Geo map** shows **400** in the sole construction row under **Not on the map**. Half of the refreshed ramp is absent from that inventory. Company/cohort totals themselves are correct; this finding does not claim they are understated.

**Correction:** refresh the unmapped row to the same ramp name, 800 MW, and Q3 guidance basis, or split it into nonoverlapping entries totaling 800 MW. Retain the unknown building allocation rather than inventing a pin. The in-memory control in the reproduction updates only this representation and leaves company, cohort, timeline, and valuation calculations unchanged. Add a regression check that this refreshed ramp is represented consistently across the site and geographic inventories.

### 2. P3 — Advance or qualify the curation date when incorporating the Q3 update

**Diff location:** `config/capacity.json:3226`; the unchanged metadata is at `config/capacity.json:3`.

The PR deliberately retains `as_of_curated: 2026-10-17` because only one company was refreshed. That field is rendered as **MW data as of 2026-10-17**, and the headline also says **Estimated MW as curated Oct 17, 2026**. Neither label describes a last-complete-roster review. The new construction assumption uses the October 20 earnings call, so the refreshed artifact presents an information cutoff three days earlier than the source it incorporates. This is a date/provenance defect, not a capacity arithmetic error.

**Reproduction:** the real publisher preserves October 17 after building the new 1,700/800 MW company row. Both older date labels were confirmed in the browser with regenerated data. The event date is independently supported by the [official earnings event page](https://ir.spacex.com/events/event-details/2026/Q3-2026-Earnings/default.aspx). The tracker design's maintenance workflow explicitly calls for bumping `as_of_curated` after a filing/announcement update (`docs/superpowers/specs/2026-07-21-capacity-tracker-design.md:195–198`).

**Correction:** advance the curation date for this refresh, or add a visible row-level update date and explicitly label the existing global date as the last full-roster review. Pin the intended date semantics in a regression check.
