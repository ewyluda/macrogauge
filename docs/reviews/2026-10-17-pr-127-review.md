## Findings, in severity order

### 1. P1 — Do not date entire campuses to the first partial delivery

**Location:** `config/capacity.json:186–190`, also `200–218`; another instance at `3098–3102`.

The new Oracle rows pair full-campus capacities with dates on which customer delivery **begins**. The publisher does not represent a gradual ramp: `_events` emits every MW in the row, and `_timeline` adds that whole amount in its assigned quarter. Consequently the new configuration schedules all 1,400 MW of Shackelford and 1,000 MW of Doña Ana in 2027Q2, then all 1,400 MW of Saline and 1,000 MW of Port Washington in 2027Q4. The saved Oracle Q4 FY26 call gives first-delivery dates, without a first-phase MW allocation. Shackelford's own new text still places full build around Q4 2028, while moving its numeric quarter from 2028Q4 to 2027Q2.

**Reproduction:** the actual publisher assigns 2,400 MW to Q2 and another 2,400 MW to Q4 2027. Setting only those four dates to null in an in-memory control removes 4,800 MW from the end-2027 curve. That is the amount given unsupported full-capacity dates, **not a claim that zero capacity will actually arrive in 2027**. The Oracle-filtered browser view reports “Dated to energize by Q4 2027 +5.0 GW” (including the separately dated 206 MW Abilene remainder), and renders the two 2.4 GW bars. See the accompanying screenshot.

Digital Realty is another concrete example: the new 288 MW Northern Virginia row puts all three buildings in 2027Q2, despite the source and its own text assigning two 96 MW buildings to H1 2027 and the third to H1 2028. A 192/96 MW split moves 96 MW from 2027Q2 to 2028Q2. Those source dates describe stabilization; they do not establish that all 288 MW are energized in H1 2027. The company's [June 29 release](https://digitalrealtytrust.gcs-web.com/news-releases/news-release-details/digital-realty-announces-purchase-blackstone-interest-three) was independently reopened and confirms the separate schedules.

**Correction:** split quantified phases into separate rows; retain null where first-phase MW cannot be established, or use a supported full-delivery quarter for the whole capacity. A first-delivery date can remain in explanatory text. Add semantic fixtures asserting that full-campus MW do not enter the cumulative curve on an unquantified first-phase date. Recheck other newly dated phased rows, including WULF CB-4 and NBIS Lappeenranta. This follows the PR's own stated phase rule.

**Evidence:** `docs/research/evidence/2026-10-17-cap-orcl-q4fy26-call.txt`, `2026-10-17-cap-orcl-q1fy27-call.txt`, `2026-10-17-cap-dlr-nova-blackstone-288mw.txt`; repro keys `oracle_full_campuses` and `dlr_phases`.

### 2. P2 — Keep new grid-power disclosures out of the critical-IT MW field

**Location:** `config/capacity.json:1123–1126` and `1161–1164`, plus the corresponding geo records.

Previously the combined Kiowa/Bundey row had null MW. This PR replaces it with Kiowa **1,600 MW** and Bundey **800 MW**, explicitly described as grid capacity, in the same numeric field used for critical-IT capacity. The publisher passes those numbers through unchanged. The capacity page labels its units as critical IT, and the map and its tables also treat this field as IT capacity. Text mentioning grid power does not change those typed units or the map's sizing and comparisons.

**Reproduction:** the new config emits 2,400 MW of explicitly grid-only capacity through the site and geo payloads. The base emits no numeric MW for these two sites. This is newly introduced contamination; it is separate from the older grid/IT mixtures acknowledged in the PR. Redbank's new 900 MW also needs a supported IT basis before use in this field, but it is not included in the 2,400 MW quantified finding.

**Impact:** users cannot compare these sites with verified critical-IT sites on the stated basis. This does **not** increase the company-level cohort totals: those use separately curated `op`/`con`/`plan`, and these rows are secured/planned rather than dated construction events.

**Correction:** leave numeric IT MW null and preserve grid MW in the description, as this PR does for several other new sites, or introduce an explicit MW-basis field and update every consumer. Do not infer a conversion without support. Add a fixture ensuring a grid-only disclosure cannot appear as measured critical-IT capacity.

**Evidence:** new config descriptions and `pipe`; `docs/research/evidence/2026-10-17-cap-iren-pipeline-zacks.txt`, `2026-10-17-cap-iren-redbank-nsw.txt`; repro key `new_iren_grid_only`.

### 3. P2 — Retain A901's disclosed full-delivery quarter

**Location:** `config/capacity.json:1462–1466`.

The new A901 row explicitly says all **67 MW** are due in **2027Q2**, but its fifth element is null. `site_quarter` treats that as a deliberate override, so the publisher never parses the date in the text and drops this project from every timeline quarter. Construction totals include the 67 MW, while the scheduled delivery view omits them even after the disclosed full-delivery date.

The [October 8 Bitdeer release](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/10/08/3377763/0/en/bitdeer-ai-expands-southeast-asia-footprint-with-a901-a-new-67mw-ai-data-center-campus-in-malaysia-active-ai-cloud-pipeline-exceeds-10-billion.html), independently reopened, distinguishes an unquantified Q1 start from full 67 MW delivery in Q2. It also explicitly identifies the figures as critical IT. An unknown Q1 tranche does not make the full Q2 amount undated.

**Reproduction:** A901 has no entry in published BTDR `tl`. Changing only its quarter to `2027Q2` in an in-memory control adds exactly 67 MW to the all/neocloud cumulative curve from that quarter onward. Its lack of customer offtake is already disclosed and is not a publisher exclusion rule.

**Correction:** use the supported full-delivery quarter for the 67 MW, while leaving the Q1 ramp unquantified. Test that an explicit full-capacity date survives publication without moving the entire amount to the initial partial-delivery quarter.

**Evidence:** `docs/research/evidence/2026-10-17-cap-btdr-a901.txt`; repro key `a901_undated`.
