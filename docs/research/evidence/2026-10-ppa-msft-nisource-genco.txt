# NiSource announces long-term agreement with Microsoft expected to increase customer savings and support Indiana's economic growth

NiSource Inc. (NYSE: NI) announced today a new long-term energy agreement with Microsoft for its previously announced northwest Indiana campus. The agreement is expected to expand customer savings while supporting economic growth across the region, reflecting NiSource’s continued focus on aligning economic development with customer affordability. Through NiSource Generation Company LLC (GenCo), the company is serving new large-load customers under a structure designed to protect existing Northern Indiana Public Service Company LLC (NIPSCO) electric customers from incremental costs and return value from large-load customers’ use of the existing electric system.

[...]

Under the agreement, Microsoft is expected to begin taking electric service under a 20-year term starting in 2028. The GenCo-owned pool portfolio will be increased by approximately 1,100 MW with NIPSCO and GenCo to provide transmission and generation infrastructure to ensure adequate assets to serve Microsoft’s data center and NIPSCO’s customers while providing a safe, reliable, and coordinated power supply. Microsoft fully funds all generation and transmission required to serve its load. These assets will upgrade NIPSCO’s electric transmission system, improving grid reliability and resilience for customers in the state of Indiana, while ensuring current residential and business customers are protected from the costs of serving the data center. In connection with the Microsoft agreement, GenCo has contracted 600 MW of battery storage, under a long-term tolling agreement. The facility will be developed and constructed by an affiliate of AGP Group. This resource will be added to GenCo’s pool resource assets designed to serve multiple large-load customers.
