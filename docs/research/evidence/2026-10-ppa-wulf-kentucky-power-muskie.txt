# Kentucky Power, TeraWulf Finalize Expanded Agreement Providing for $100 Million in Customer Benefits
# Source: https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/kentucky-power-terawulf-finalize-expanded-agreement-providing-for-100-million-in-customer-benefits-302898378.html
# Publisher: Kentucky Power, an AEP company (press release, PR Newswire)
# Date: 2026-10-05
# Retrieved 2026-10-17 (curl + HTML strip, whitespace collapsed; excerpt = the release body through the filing plan)

Kentucky Power has finalized an amended agreement that would double its contracted electric demand with TeraWulf Inc. (Nasdaq: WULF ) while delivering significant long-term benefits for customers and communities across eastern Kentucky. If approved by the Kentucky Public Service Commission, the amended agreement is expected to provide $100 million in winter bill credits funded by TeraWulf for Kentucky Power's residential customers over the first 10 years of the contract. For a typical residential customer, the bill credits could amount to approximately $25 per month during the winter heating season, with credits beginning in 2029. The expanded agreement would increase contracted electric demand at TeraWulf's Muskie Data campus in Grayson, Kentucky, from 500 megawatts (MW) to 1 gigawatt (GW). It also advances planned delivery of the second 500-MW phase from 2030 to 2029, subject to Commission approval and Kentucky Power's construction schedule. The campus's planned expansion is expected to generate new economic activity through infrastructure investment, construction activity, permanent jobs and new tax revenue for local communities. These investments will help support the continued modernization of Kentucky Power's system. In addition to paying all applicable service charges, TeraWulf has agreed to pay the estimated financing costs associated with construction of Kentucky Power's planned 760-MW combined-cycle generation facility at Big Sandy, helping ensure the costs associated with serving increased demand are not shifted to existing customers. "At Kentucky Power, we are working to bring new businesses and economic opportunity to eastern Kentucky, creating meaningful benefits for the customers and communities we serve," said Cindy Wiseman, president and chief operating officer of Kentucky Power. "By providing a decade's worth of direct residential bill credits, supporting critical energy infrastructure investments and helping ensure the costs associated with new demand are paid by the customers driving that growth, this agreement demonstrates how economic development can create lasting value for our customers and communities." "This agreement shows how large-scale investment can support both economic growth and meaningful benefits for existing utility customers," said Paul Prager, chairman and chief executive officer of TeraWulf. "Kentucky Power has been a strong partner in advancing Muskie's development. Expanding contracted power to 1 GW and bringing the second phase forward to 2029 positions us to meet strong customer demand and bring lasting investment to eastern Kentucky."

# TeraWulf Expands Infrastructure Platform with Acquisition of 1+ GW Eastern Kentucky HPC Campus (the original 500 MW service agreement)

The Muskie Data Campus, acquired from Industrial Equity Partners (“IEP”), is expected to support more than 1 gigawatt (“GW”) of data center capacity over time. Delivery of the initial 500 megawatts (“MW”) is expected to ramp beginning in the second half of 2028, with an additional 500 MW targeted for delivery in the second half of 2030.

[...]

Kentucky Power, an AEP Company, is constructing a 345 kV substation connected to the existing 765 kV transmission network, providing redundant, utility-scale power infrastructure designed to support the full 1+ GW campus. Transmission infrastructure and energy service agreements were executed concurrently with the acquisition pursuant to the applicable Industrial General Service tariff structure for large loads, establishing a clear pathway to long-term, large-scale power delivery.
