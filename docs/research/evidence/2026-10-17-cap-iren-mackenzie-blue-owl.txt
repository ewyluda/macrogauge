[…] NEW YORK, Aug. 28, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Blue Owl Capital Inc. (NYSE: OWL) ("Blue Owl"), a leading alternative asset manager, today announced that funds managed by Blue Owl led a $2.4 billion compute equipment financing for IREN Limited (NASDAQ: IREN) ("IREN"), a vertically integrated AI Cloud platform delivering data centers, compute, and software for AI training and inference. The financing comprises a $1.2 billion senior secured term loan and $1.2 billion of senior secured notes, with proceeds to fund IREN's purchase of air-cooled NVIDIA Accelerated Computing Infrastructure including NVIDIA Blackwell Ultra GPUs for IREN's Mackenzie data center campus in British Columbia, Canada.
