[…] today announced that it has entered into an 8-year data center services agreement for A103, a 60MW AI Cloud data center facility on the same Cyberjaya, Malaysia campus as its A101 and A102 facilities. […]

[…] A103 is designed for liquid-cooled, rack-scale NVIDIA systems, including the next-generation NVIDIA Vera Rubin platform, with energization expected in the first quarter of 2028. A103 is located on the existing Cyberjaya campus allowing Bitdeer AI to leverage established power, liquid cooling and networking to shorten the path to energization. Together with the A101 and A102 facilities, the Cyberjaya campus will represent approximately 71.5MW of AI Cloud data center capacity. […]

[…] With the addition of the A103 facility, Bitdeer AI's secured AI Cloud data center capacity — capacity owned or under an executed data center services agreement — totals approximately 333.5MW across facilities in Malaysia, Norway and the United States, representing approximately 95% of its target of up to 350MW to be delivered by the first quarter of 2028. All megawatt figures in this release refer to critical IT load. […]

[…] No offtake commitments have been entered into with respect to A103 capacity as of the date of this press release. […]
