[…] NiSource Inc. (NYSE:NI) announced today a new long-term energy agreement with Microsoft for its previously announced northwest Indiana campus. […]
[…] Under the agreement, Microsoft is expected to begin taking electric service under a 20-year term starting in 2028. The GenCo-owned pool portfolio will be increased by approximately 1,100 MW with NIPSCO and GenCo to provide transmission and generation infrastructure to ensure adequate assets to serve Microsoft's data center and NIPSCO's customers while providing a safe, reliable, and coordinated power supply. Microsoft fully funds all generation and transmission required to serve its load. […]
[…] In connection with the Microsoft agreement, GenCo has contracted 600 MW of battery storage, under a long-term tolling agreement. The facility will be developed and constructed by an affiliate of AGP Group. This resource will be added to GenCo's pool resource assets designed to serve multiple large-load customers. […]
[…] Further operational and financial details on the agreement and NiSource's broader approach to supporting data center growth will be shared on the company's third quarter earnings call. […]
