English gloss: "Alibaba Cloud's Brazil data center officially entered service. The Brazil region has two availability zones offering compute, storage, database and other cloud services; enterprise AI services will follow. It is Alibaba Cloud's second region in Latin America, after Mexico (Feb 2025). Alibaba Cloud's global infrastructure now reaches 31 regions and 106 availability zones."
