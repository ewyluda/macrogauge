Google and Elementl Power are collaborating to advance nuclear energy site development. May 07, 2025 Google is collaborating with advanced nuclear project developer Elementl Power through a strategic agreement to accelerate the development of clean, reliable energy. Google will provide early-stage capital to help Elementl prepare three potential sites in the U.S. for advanced nuclear projects, each aiming for at least 600 MW of capacity. This agreement is part of our continued work to source 24/7 baseload energy to support our operations and strengthen power grids. It also helps Elementl advance its goal of bringing significant nuclear capacity online by 2035. This innovative approach links capital investment directly with the growing demand for clean baseload power, with Google having the option for commercial off-take once projects are complete.
